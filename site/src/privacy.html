Privacy

GradeGuide collects nothing about you or your child. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

The apps

The GradeGuide apps for Android, iPhone and iPad carry the whole site inside them. They don't connect to the internet and send nothing anywhere. Links to other sites open in your browser.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits, as any web host does; see GitHub's privacy statement. GradeGuide adds no tracking of its own.

On your device

The site and the apps remember a display preference, such as whether the sources list is open, in your browser's or app's own storage. It stays on your device.

Feedback

Feedback links open a public GitHub issue, which needs a GitHub account. Anything you write there is public, so please don't include your child's name or anything personal.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/gradeguide/gradeguide.
